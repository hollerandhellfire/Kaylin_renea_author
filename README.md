## About the Author

**Angela Smith**, writing professionally as **Kaylin Renea**, is the creator of the Holler & Hellfire Universe, an interconnected storytelling universe built around compelling characters, immersive settings, complex relationships, and unforgettable narratives.

Her creative vision brings together original literary works and interconnected stories, providing readers with opportunities to explore the H&H Universe through books and other storytelling experiences.

As both an author and universe creator, Angela oversees the creative identity, storytelling direction, and continued development of her literary projects.

## The Holler & Hellfire Universe

The **Holler & Hellfire Universe** is an original creative and literary property developed by Angela Smith.

The universe is designed to support interconnected stories, characters, worlds, and creative projects across multiple formats.

Its development includes:
